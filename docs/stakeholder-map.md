## 2. Detailed Stakeholder Profiles

### 2.1 Talenta Vokasi Team (Tim Kerja Pemagangan LN Pusat)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manages national-level operational coordination between regional BPVP balai, private LPKs, and international receiving bodies.
  - Consolidates applicant numbers across 38 provinces.
  - Prepares draft ministerial decrees (*Surat Keputusan*), circular letters (*Surat Edaran*), and operational technical guidelines (*Juknis*).
  - Troubleshoots participant issues and handles public escalations.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Overwhelmed by endless spreadsheet consolidation: receives 20+ separate Excel files from regional balai and LPKs, each with different formatting, column orders, and spelling errors.
  - No single source of truth: unable to instantly tell Echelon leadership how many trainees are currently abroad or in centralized training.
  - High risk of lost documents and delayed communication due to reliance on personal WhatsApp accounts.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Microsoft Excel (desktop), Google Drive, WhatsApp Web, Microsoft Word, Gmail / webmail Kemnaker.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Master participant dispatch ledgers, draft ministerial decrees, aggregated national quota tracking sheets, coordination meeting minutes (*Notula*).

---

### 2.2 BPVP (Balai Pelatihan Vokasi dan Produktivitas Daerah)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Serves as the physical front-desk for regional candidate registration and paper folder collection.
  - Manages regional document verification and initial administrative screening.
  - Organizes local physical fitness examinations (running, push-ups, sit-ups) and paper-based math tests in cooperation with local military/police instructors.
  - Answers daily walk-in and telephone inquiries from anxious candidates and parents.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Physical archive storage crisis: offices overflowing with cardboard boxes containing thousands of paper folders (*map snelhefter*).
  - Manual verification fatigue: inspecting thousands of photocopies by hand leads to human error and eye strain.
  - Candidate contact breakdown: candidates frequently change SIM cards or provide non-active WhatsApp numbers, causing missed test appointments.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Paper checklists, physical rubber stamps (*stempel dinas*), cardboard scoring stencils, local desktop Excel, landline phones.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Signed physical verification checklists, regional selection score sheets, physical archive boxes, local candidate pass lists.

---

### 2.3 BBPVP (Balai Besar Pelatihan Vokasi dan Produktivitas Pembina)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Acts as the primary residential hub for Stage 3 (Pelatihan Terpusat / Centralized Immersion Training) for 2 to 3 months prior to departure (e.g., BBPVP Bandung, BBPVP Serang, BBPVP Medan, BBPVP Semarang).
  - Manages dormitory boarding intake, meals, medical monitoring, and cultural adaptation regimens.
  - Oversees intensive language training (Japanese N4/N5, German A1/B1) and industrial etiquette (*5S / Kaizen*).
- **Pain Points:**
  - Dormitory capacity bottlenecks: sudden batch date changes or quota expansions lead to bed shortages.
  - Paper-based attendance and grading: instructors record language scores on personal notebooks; consolidating attrition rates (dropouts/illness) back to central Kemnaker is slow.
  - Inadequate medical tracking: discovering pre-existing medical conditions mid-training that were missed during initial regional screening.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Dormitory room allocation spreadsheets, whiteboard daily rosters, paper attendance binders, WhatsApp instructor groups.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Centralized training completion certificates, attrition/drop-out logbooks, final pre-departure readiness evaluations.

---

### 2.4 LPK / Sending Organization (SO)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Prepares candidates in the regions with preliminary language instruction before applying to Kemnaker programs.
  - Sponsors candidate batches and coordinates interview schedules with foreign accepting organizations (AO / host companies).
  - Prepares visa documentation, air tickets, and pre-departure administrative requirements.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Opacity in selection schedules: uncertainty regarding when Kemnaker will open the next batch registration or announce selection results.
  - Duplicate manual paperwork: having to submit the same candidate documents multiple times to both local BPVP, central Kemnaker, and foreign immigration bureaus.
  - Communication gap: dependent on informal personal connections with Kemnaker staff to get status updates on batch decrees.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Internal student spreadsheets, Google Drive, WhatsApp groups with candidates, email correspondence with Japanese/German partners.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Candidate recommendation rosters, preliminary language certificates (NAT-TEST / JLPT), visa application dossiers, flight departure schedules.

---

### 2.5 Program Managers (Subkoordinator / Penanggung Jawab Batch Negara)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Manages operational execution for specific bilateral country corridors (e.g., Subkoordinator Magang Jepang, Subkoordinator Magang Jerman).
  - Directly interfaces with foreign bilateral counterparties (IM Japan, IHK/AHK Jerman, HRDK Korea).
  - Formulates batch schedules, monitors quota fulfillment, and plans departure ceremonies.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Constant fire-fighting: spending 70% of working hours chasing down missing spreadsheets and resolving discrepancies between regional balai data and foreign partner lists.
  - High vulnerability to administrative delays: waiting days for physical memo routing and wet signatures while foreign visa deadlines approach.
  - Lack of longitudinal data: difficult to answer partner inquiries regarding historical trainee performance or alumni return rates.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Multiple fragmented Excel workbooks, WhatsApp, Zoom, physical memo circulation slips (*lembar disposisi*).
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Bilateral batch execution plans, quota allocation proposals, participant visa recommendation letters, dispatch decrees.

---

### 2.6 Candidate (Calon Peserta Magang)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Prepares personal identity documents, legalized school diplomas, police certificates (SKCK), and medical check-ups.
  - Fills registration forms and attends in-person selection tests at designated BPVP balai.
  - Commits to intensive 2–3 months residential training away from family.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Anxiety and information darkness: after submitting documents, candidates wait weeks with zero visibility into whether their application was accepted, under review, or rejected.
  - High financial burden: paying out-of-pocket for travel to distant testing centers, medical check-ups, and photocopies/meterai with no guarantee of transparent feedback.
  - Scams and predatory brokers (*calo pemagangan*): false promises from unauthorized entities claiming to have "insider access" to Kemnaker selection quotas.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Smartphone, WhatsApp, Instagram, physical document folders.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Physical application dossier, completed test answer sheets, signed agreement contracts, boarding attendance.

---

### 2.7 Director (Direktur Eselon II — Bina Penyelenggaraan Pelatihan Vokasi & Pemagangan)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - Sets national policy, bilateral quotas, and strategic program priorities for vocational talent export.
  - Signs statutory decrees (SK Penetapan Kelulusan & Pemberangkatan Peserta).
  - Reports to the Dirjen Binalavotas and Menteri Ketenagakerjaan on national vocational apprenticeship metrics.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Data latency: receiving outdated statistics compiled days prior; unable to get real-time operational counts on an executive mobile device.
  - Signature backlog: piles of physical paper folders waiting for wet ink signatures whenever returning from official domestic or overseas travel.
  - Reputational and legal risk: exposure to public criticism or legal challenge if selection errors or quota deviations occur in regional balai.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Executive paper briefing folders, printed PowerPoint slide decks, official wet ink fountain pen and stamp.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Signed ministerial decrees, bilateral policy directives, annual performance reports (LAKIP/Renja).

---

### 2.8 External Partners (IM Japan, Foreign Host Companies, Embassies)

- **Responsibilities:**
  - IM Japan / Accepting Organizations (AO): Provides industrial internship placements, sets job skill requirements, and pays trainee monthly stipends.
  - Foreign Immigration & Embassies: Issues Certificates of Eligibility (COE) and internship visas.
  - Indonesian Embassies (KBRI/KJRI): Provides consular supervision and worker protection in host nations.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Format inconsistency: receiving candidate rosters in non-standardized Excel layouts with mixed Japanese/Indonesian naming conventions.
  - Latency in candidate document submission: delays in Kemnaker selection decrees impact host company production planning in Japan or Germany.
  - Limited post-placement coordination: lack of automated incident escalation when a trainee experiences workplace grievances.
- **Current Tools:**
  - Email, international courier mail (DHL/FedEx for physical visa dossiers), Zoom video calls, proprietary partner portals.
- **Required Outputs:**
  - Industrial placement quotas, host company acceptance notices, Certificate of Eligibility (COE), consular endorsements.
